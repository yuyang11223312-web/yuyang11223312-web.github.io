“双碳”目标下临港新片区湿地演化与固碳潜力提升
Wetland Dynamics and Carbon Sequestration Potential in Shanghai Lingang

研究内容 / Research Focus:围绕临港新片区滨海湿地演化与固碳潜力评估，梳理湿地类型、生态修复情景与碳效益之间的关系。

This project examines coastal wetland dynamics and carbon sequestration potential in Lingang, focusing on relationships among wetland types, restoration scenarios, and carbon benefits.

技术方法 / Methods:结合文献数据整理、遥感数据处理、GEE 与 GIS 空间分析，核查湿地面积和碳储量估算依据。

Methods include literature-based data compilation, remote-sensing data processing, and spatial analysis with GEE and GIS to examine wetland areas and carbon-stock estimates.

当前进展 / Current Progress:正在核实研究区数据与文献来源，并整理固碳潜力情景分析所需的基础数据。

Current work focuses on verifying study-area data and literature sources and organizing inputs for carbon-sequestration scenario analysis.
